# Customer data and privacy

Customers run their businesses and their lives on our products. Our promise to them is simple: their information is confidential, even from us. Our [privacy policy](https://37signals.com/policies/privacy/) and [DPA](https://37signals.com/policies/privacy/dpa) spell out that promise publicly; this page covers what it means for how you work day to day.

## What counts as customer data

Everything a customer puts into our products, and everything our systems record about them: the content itself (messages, to-dos, documents, files, chats, email), and account information (names, email addresses, IP logs, billing details). If it belongs to a customer, treat it as confidential.

## Only look when the work requires it

Don’t access customer data unless the task in front of you requires it – a support case with the customer’s permission, an abuse investigation, a production incident. No browsing, no curiosity. Production console access is audited with [console1984](https://github.com/basecamp/console1984): you state a reason, the session is recorded, and touching sensitive data takes an extra, logged justification. Logging into a customer’s account through Queenbee follows [its own rule](https://github.com/basecamp/handbook/blob/master/our-internal-systems.md#queenbee): only with the customer’s explicit permission, never preemptively.

## Keep it inside our systems

Customer data lives in our applications and internal tools, and nowhere else. Don’t copy it into outside tools or services, and don’t put it on personal devices – the same line drawn in [Managing work devices](https://github.com/basecamp/handbook/blob/master/managing-work-devices.md): work data stays on work machines. If you need a snippet to debug something or hand off a case, take the minimum and delete it when you’re done.

## If something goes wrong

If you think customer data may have been exposed, lost, or accessed by someone who shouldn’t have – including by your own mistake – say so immediately. Tell your manager and the SIP team right away. We’ve committed in [our DPA](https://37signals.com/policies/privacy/dpa) (§7) to notify affected customers without undue delay, and that clock starts as soon as any of us becomes aware. Speed and candor beat embarrassment, every time.

## Privacy questions

[privacy@37signals.com](mailto:privacy@37signals.com) is our designated privacy contact. If a customer asks about their data rights, a deletion request, or anything privacy-related that support can’t answer from our published policies, route it there. Your own privacy questions can go there too.
